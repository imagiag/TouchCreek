Creek monitor

waiting: open the emulator in another tab, or Connect the Touch 2
How to use
  1. Emulator: press Monitor in the emulator; this page follows it automatically (same browser).
  2. Touch 2: build the monitor firmware with make -C daisy monitor, flash daisy/build-monitor/CreekTouch-monitor.bin (its USB sends this report instead of MIDI), plug in, click Connect Touch 2 and pick Creek (or "Daisy Seed").
  3. Hunting an artefact: watch Layers to see which sound is active when you hear it, mute layers in Diagnostics (left switch down, P03–P07) and check Last pressed for what changed just before.
  4. Worst-case CPU (Touch 2 only): Diagnostics + P09 stress test. The brief asks for the worst 1 ms block below 70 %.
