Creek monitor

live numbers from the Touch 2 over USB (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Build the monitor firmware with make -C daisy monitor (it isn't part of the releases; the normal build uses USB for MIDI instead), flash daisy/build-monitor/CreekTouch-monitor.bin and plug the Touch 2 into this computer by USB.
  2. Click Connect and pick Creek (or "Daisy Seed") in the list.
  3. Play normally to see everyday load. For the worst case: left switch down (Diagnostics), tap P09 (LED blinks fast, the creek goes wild) and let it run. Tap P09 again or leave Diagnostics to stop.
  4. The brief asks for the worst audio callback below 70 % of its 1 ms deadline.
