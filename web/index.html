Controls
ControlFunction
Left fader S36Level: silent at the bottom, 0 dB at about 70 %, +6 dB at the top (as TouchPulsar).
Right fader S37Roughness: smooth channel → rocky interruptions: more turbulence, deeper eddies, more uneven splashes, more surface ticks.
S31Scale: fine shallow trickle → larger stream body (bubble size 0.6–1.8×, lower pitch).
S32Aeration: sparse bubbling → bubbly white water. Fully left = no bubbles.
S33Flow: still → trickle → lively creek. Fully left fades to silence.
S34Width: mono fully left, natural at noon, wider fully right.
S30, S35Reserved.
Left switchup = Presets · centre = Notes · down = Diagnostics
Right switchdown = Hold the slow creek state (bubbles keep happening).
PresetsP03 Quiet trickle · P04 Rocky creek · P05 Small cascade · P06 Deeper run (250 ms glide)
NotesP03–P09 = the scale (default C major pentatonic from C5). Tap = a tuned drop with a little bubbly tail; hold = the creek's own bubbles gather onto the held notes (up to 4). P00 / P02 octave down / up. P11 + P00 / P02 / P03–P09 = scale (same 7 scales as TouchPulsar). P10 + note = root.
DiagnosticsP03 Flow bed (the noise wash, switched off for now), P04 Bubbles, P05 Eddies, P06 Falls, P07 Surface: tap to mute / unmute. P08 = all on. P09 = stress test (worst-case settings with sudden jumps and loud note bursts, LED blinks fast) for measuring CPU with the monitor.
LEDMode change: 1 / 2 / 3 blinks (presets / notes / diagnostics). Preset number 1–4; scale number 1–7; octave; 2 = layer on, 1 = muted. Otherwise flickers with notes and louder bubbles.

Soft pickup: after a preset, the four sound knobs (Flow, Roughness, Scale, Aeration) keep their place but no longer match the sound. The gold arc shows the value in use, the white pointer where the knob is. A knob takes over again once you move it a little.

Layers: bubbles (the main voice), eddies (slow swells that make the rock wake surge: no sound of their own), falls (a soft low plop at the drop, then bubbly settling), surface (sparse quiet high ticks). The noise wash is off for now.

Mouse: drag knobs up/down (Alt = fine), drag faders, click switches (top / middle / bottom), click higher on a pad for a harder hit. Keys: D left switch, H right switch, Space audio; presets 1–4; notes 1–7, Z/X octave; diagnostics 1–5, 6 all.
