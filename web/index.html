P03 – P06Presets: Quiet trickle, Rocky creek, Small cascade, Deeper run (250 ms glide)P03 Flow, P04 Bubbles, P05 Eddies, P06 Falls, P07 Surface: tap to mute / unmute. P08 = all on.
LEDBlinks the preset number (1–4); 2 blinks = layer on, 1 = muted, 3 = all on. Otherwise flickers with louder bubbles (dark flickers in diagnostics).

Soft pickup: after a preset, the macro knobs keep their place but no longer match the sound. The gold arc shows the value in use, the white pointer where the knob is. A knob takes over again once you move it a little. Eddies, falls and surface detail arrive in Phase 2.

Mouse: drag knobs up/down (Alt = fine), drag faders, click switches (top / middle / bottom). Keys: 1–4 presets (diagnostics: 1–5 layers, 6 all), D left switch, H right switch, Space audio.
